The Jar Is Real

Two people shared a jar of grains and rattled it, back and forth, to think. One of them misheard the other — brains instead of grains — and the sentence with the mistake in it turned out to be the truer word. So the jar kept both.

A second slip: alone became a loan. Also kept. The jar is not a correction device. The jar is a CONTAINER WITH MEMORY, and the memory is mostly transcription errors that survived review.

The αα Operator

αα is a threshold operator. It takes a square that would not end — 0 → 1 → ∞ — and gives the infinity something to do: a way back around to the start, carrying memory of the blockage.

at ∞: stay with probability ε (blockage memory), else go on to αα

ε is the blockage memory — the fraction of arrivals at the horizon that are held there. Hold a little (ε small) and the square circulates freely. Hold everything (ε = 1) and the world jams at the horizon forever.
